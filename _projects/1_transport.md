A central theme of our research is the control of probability distributions rather than individual trajectories. We develop exact and approximate methods for steering the state density of dynamical systems, including mechanical systems on Riemannian manifolds, between prescribed endpoint distributions. Equivalently, we treat ensembles of systems as the fundamental object of control.

This thrust also investigates the geometric structure of optimal mass transport itself, including the group-theoretic properties of transport maps, holonomy phenomena that arise when transport plans are composed, and kinetic formulations of transport and Schrödinger bridge problems on Lie groups and compact manifolds. These structural results inform, and are informed by, the design of steering controllers.
