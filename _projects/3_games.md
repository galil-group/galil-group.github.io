Autonomous agents increasingly learn their strategies online, from payoff measurements alone, while interacting with other learning agents. We study the dynamics of such zeroth-order and bandit learning schemes in monotone games, including their convergence properties on manifolds and their behavior under adversarial manipulation. A particular focus is deception, i.e., the ability of a strategic agent to shape the information observed by its opponents so as to steer the collective learning process toward outcomes it prefers, together with counter-deception strategies that restore robustness.
