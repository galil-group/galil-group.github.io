On manifolds and in obstructed environments, topological obstructions rule out globally stabilizing continuous feedback. We design hybrid and synergistic feedback laws that overcome these obstructions, providing global, robust guarantees for stabilization and navigation. Current work addresses dynamic environments, in which the obstacles themselves move according to their own dynamics, and the interplay between these geometric methods and the transport-based ensemble control developed in the lab's other thrusts.
