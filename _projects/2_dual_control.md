A controller acting under model uncertainty faces a fundamental tension. Its actions must simultaneously regulate the system and excite it enough to learn the unknown model, the classical dual control problem. We approach this problem geometrically, combining the Wasserstein geometry of state distributions with information-geometric structure on the space of models. The objective is a principled synthesis theory in which exploration and regulation are traded off along geodesics of an appropriate joint geometry, rather than through heuristic probing signals.
