research

Research thrusts.

Optimal transport and density steering

Steering probability distributions of dynamical systems on manifolds, and the geometry of transport maps.

Model-free control and optimization

Extremum seeking and zeroth-order methods that steer systems to optimal operating points using measurements alone.

Geometric and hybrid feedback

Feedback design that respects topology, including hybrid controllers for global stabilization and obstacle avoidance.
